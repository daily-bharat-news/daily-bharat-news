‘jana nayagan’ box office collection day 8: thalapathy vijay ki film ki kamai jaari

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 05:59 AM · स्रोत जाँचा गया

thalapathy vijay aur pooja hegde ki film ‘jana nayagan’ ka box office par safar jaari hai. aathve din is film ne bharat mein 3.98 crore rupaye ki kamai ki hai, jisase iska kul collection sthir bana hua hai. isake alawa, is film ne duniya bhar mein 260.25 crore rupaye ka ankda par kar liya hai. tamil nadu mein bhi is film ne 100 crore rupaye se adhik ki kamai karake ek naya record banaya hai, halaanki kuch reports mein iski raftaar ko lekar alag-alag daave kiye gaye hain.
